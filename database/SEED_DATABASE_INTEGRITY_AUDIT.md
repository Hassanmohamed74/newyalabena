### 8.2 Conditional Safety Issues

### 8.4 Overall Idempotency Verdict

**Safe to rerun under specific conditions** — Both seed files are idempotent when run individually. However, running v3 after v2.1 may create duplicate enrollments (student already enrolled in group from v2.1). Running either seed multiple times is safe due to ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING.
